What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-reference sheet used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. A useful entry records the product URL, selected variant, price currency, measurements and any available QC reference. A spreadsheet listing alone does not verify a seller or product.

02 / Oopbuy shopping guide

Four checks before
a link becomes a choice.

A repeatable comparison method for sneakers, clothing, headwear and accessories.

01

Save the exact item.

Keep the original product link, seller reference, selected color and size together. Similar photos can appear across different listings.

02

Measure for your fit.

Compare garment measurements with an item you already own. Record the units and whether a dimension is measured flat or around the body.

03

Inspect the evidence.

Check available QC photos for the actual variant. Look at visible construction, shape and finishing, and note anything the images cannot resolve.

04

Compare the full cost.

Separate the item price from shipping, applicable fees and possible import charges. Mark estimates clearly and confirm them before payment.

to know first.

Short answers for readers building a product shortlist or using a shopping spreadsheet for the first time.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides category links and a comparison guide. It does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or connect to an Oopbuy account.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you own. Check the measurement method, units, chest width, length, shoulders and sleeve length rather than relying only on a size label.

What should I check in QC photos?

Check that the photos match the selected item and variant. Inspect visible stitching, shape, color, labels and included accessories. QC photos cannot establish authenticity, long-term durability or a guaranteed fit.

How do I estimate the total shopping cost?

Add the item price, applicable domestic shipping, service and payment fees, international shipping and any applicable import charges. Confirm the currency and distinguish quoted amounts from estimates.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide. Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès are referenced for identification and editorial context. No affiliation, endorsement or product authentication is claimed.
